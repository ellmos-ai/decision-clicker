3. GOVERNANCE & RUNTIME INVARIANTS (10 Invariants)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- INV-LOCAL-01: 100% Local-First & Zero-Egress Privacy (Loopback only, zero outbound calls)
- INV-CANON-02: Single Data Canon Guarantee (TO-DECIDE-USER.txt remains sole authority)
- INV-NOELEV-03: Unprivileged Mode / RunAsInvoker (Never requires admin/root rights)
- INV-ONEDOC-04: One-Document Active Contract (Only unanswered, decision-ready entries active)
- INV-BACKUP-05: Byte-for-Byte Pre-Write Backup (Timestamped backup before every mutation)
- INV-NOWRITE-06: Never Overwrite Existing Decisions (Only placeholder markers can be answered)
- INV-UNDO-07: Provenance-Guarded Reversible Undo (Append-only audit trail retention)
- INV-LOCK-08: Foreign Lock & Multi-Agent Resilienz (Fail-closed on LOCK*.txt)
- INV-CROSS-09: Cross-Platform Operating Parity (Identical behavior across Win/Linux/macOS)
