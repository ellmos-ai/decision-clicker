2. TARGET PERSONAS & VALUE PROPOSITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona 1: Autonomous Agent Fleet Operators & Multi-Agent Swarm Architects
  * Pain: AI agents make critical changes without structured human gating or leave opaque JSON states.
  * Solution: File-based single data canon (TO-DECIDE-USER.txt) with reversible provenance-guarded undo.
- Persona 2: DevOps, SRE & Local Infrastructure Governance Engineers
  * Pain: External SaaS approvals fail in air-gapped or offline development environments.
  * Solution: 100% Local-First & Zero-Egress (INV-LOCAL-01) with unprivileged RunAsInvoker operation.
- Persona 3: Privacy-Conscious Enterprise AI & Compliance Officers
  * Pain: Sending decision context to cloud webhooks violates GDPR/HIPAA zero-egress boundaries.
  * Solution: Loopback-only binding (127.0.0.1:8096), zero outbound telemetry, byte-preserving backups.
- Persona 4: Local-First Desktop & Human-in-the-Loop Workflow Developers
  * Pain: Overcomplicated approval databases with complex migrations and schema drift.
  * Solution: Plain text and markdown files as permanent records; disposable index caches.
